Put your collateral to work, and keep control of it.

Deposit assets into the pool. They stay there until you take them out, and they count toward what you can borrow. Nothing is locked away in a vault you cannot see.

  • Collateral is valued in USD by the protocol's own oracle.
  • Fee-on-transfer tokens are supported. The pool counts what it really receives.
  • Withdraw any time your health factor allows it.

Borrow

Borrow vUSD against what you hold, with a clear limit.

The more collateral you add, the more you can borrow. Your health factor tells you how safe you are. Above 1 is safe. At or below 1, the position can be liquidated.

  • Rates rise as the pool fills up, so borrowing is cheap when there is spare liquidity.
  • A safety buffer keeps small price moves from putting you at risk.
  • See the health factor after your action before you confirm it.

How it works

Three steps from wallet to borrowing.

1

Supply collateral

Deposit an asset into the pool. It counts toward your borrowing power as soon as it lands.

2

Borrow against it

Borrow vUSD up to your limit. Your health factor updates with every block.

3

Repay and get it back

Repay everything, including interest, and every asset you supplied comes back to your wallet.
